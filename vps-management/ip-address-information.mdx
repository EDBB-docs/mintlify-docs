The **Network** tab contains the IPv4 addresses, IPv6 allocation, gateways and DNS resolvers for your VPS. Copy the values from your own service before changing its operating-system network configuration.

## Open Network

Sign in to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php), open **Services → My Services**, select your VPS and choose **Network**.

## IPv4 address, subnet mask and gateway

Use the **IP Address**, **Subnet Mask** and **Gateway** shown for your service. The primary address is marked **Primary IP**. Additional addresses must also be configured inside the operating system.

<Frame caption="Copy all three values from your own VPS. The example address is not usable on your service.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/guide-batch/ipv4-detail.png" alt="IPv4 address, subnet mask and gateway in the Network tab" />
  </div>
</Frame>

**Add IP +** opens the component-upgrade page. Follow [Order additional IPv4 addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/order-additional-ip-address) to review the available quantity and charge. To replace an existing primary address, see [Change an IP address](/faq/ip-addresses/change-ip-address).

## IPv6 allocation and gateway

The panel shows an assigned **/64 equivalent** range. Choose an address within that allocation and configure it with a **/48 interface prefix** so it can reach the displayed gateway. The /48 setting does not expand your assigned allocation.

See [Configure IPv6](/vps-management/ipv6-configuration) for Linux commands and persistent settings.

## DNS resolvers

The **Nameservers** section is below the address configuration. It lists recommended public IPv4 and IPv6 resolvers; enter the chosen DNS settings in your operating system or its network configuration tool.

<Frame caption="Scroll down within Network to find the DNS1 and DNS2 values.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/guide-batch/dns.png" alt="Nameservers table with recommended IPv4 and IPv6 DNS resolvers" />
  </div>
</Frame>

Open the [VNC console](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server) before changing networking. Preserve working IPv4 settings, use the correct interface name, and verify connectivity before closing your existing connection.
