## Bring Your Own ISO

<Info>
  Uploading a custom ISO lets you boot recovery toolkits, bespoke installers, or
  legacy operating systems that are not part of the EDBB library. The upload is
  temporary—plan to install or capture data within the retention window.
</Info>

## Quick Health Check


###  Direct Download Link

Host the ISO on a direct HTTPS URL without authentication, captchas, or
    one-time tokens. Do not make sensitive media publicly accessible to satisfy this requirement; ask support for an appropriate transfer method instead.


###  Maintenance Window

Selecting the force-reset/boot option reboots the VPS. Schedule a maintenance slot so users
    aren’t surprised by downtime.


###  Console Access

Keep the portal console (or your VNC client) ready—you’ll need it to interact
    with the installer once the VPS boots from the image.

## Upload the ISO to the Control Panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Installation → ISO Boot">
    Sign in to the <a href="https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">EDBB Control Panel</a>, open your VPS, and switch to <strong>Installation</strong>. Choose the <strong>ISO Boot</strong> tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the ISO URL">
    In <strong>Download ISO from URL</strong>, paste the direct link (the documentation-only example <code>https://cdn.example.com/os/custom.iso</code> must be replaced with your real image URL), then click <strong>Download ISO from another server</strong>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the transfer">
    The Control Panel downloads the file into your account. Wait for the transfer to finish, then find the downloaded file in the ISO selector.

## Mount and Boot the ISO

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to ISO Boot">
    Stay on the <strong>Installation</strong> view and open the <strong>ISO Boot</strong> sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your custom file">
    Pick the uploaded ISO from the dropdown list and enable <strong>Force reset & boot from the selected ISO</strong>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mount & follow the installer">
    Click <strong>Mount ISO</strong>. The VPS reboots into the image—use the console to work through the installer or recovery environment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Retention - Cleanup

- Custom ISOs stay available for **24 hours**. Re-upload them if you need another session later.
- Unmount the ISO once you finish. Unmounting detaches the media; it is not proof that a retained copy has been deleted. Ask support if sensitive media needs earlier removal.
- Keep local copies—expired files are purged automatically and cannot be restored.

<Warning>
  Downloads that fail usually indicate an inaccessible URL. Verify the link by
  running <code>curl -I &lt;your-link&gt;</code> from an external network and ensure
  it returns HTTP 200 without redirects.
</Warning>
