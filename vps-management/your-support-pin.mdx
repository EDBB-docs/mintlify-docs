Your Support PIN helps EDBB verify your service when handling support requests. Find it on the VPS details page and share it only through an official support channel when requested.

<span id="locate-your-support-pin" />

## Find the PIN in the VPS Control Panel

Sign in to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php), open **Services**, and select the VPS you need help with. On the **Dashboard**, look in the service-details card on the right.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the Support PIN row">
    Find **Support PIN** between **CD-ROM** and **Installed OS**. On a narrow screen, scroll to the service-details card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the PIN">
    Use the **copy icon** immediately to the right of the PIN. If copying is unavailable, select the displayed value and copy it manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the PIN for the correct VPS">
    Check the service ID at the top of the card before sharing the PIN with EDBB support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find the PIN in Your Welcome Email

You can also find the PIN in the **Plan Details** section of your VPS credentials email, alongside the KVM ID and server information. Search for **EDBB: Your Linux VPS Credentials** or **EDBB: Your Windows VPS Credentials**. See [Welcome email](/check-your-inbox).

## When You May Need the Support PIN

EDBB support may ask for the PIN to verify your service before handling a sensitive request. Include the VPS ID and a clear description of the issue, then provide the PIN when requested through the official support conversation.

## Security Recommendations

### Keep it private

Treat the PIN as confidential. Remove it from screenshots, public posts and shared logs. If you save it, use a password manager or another access-controlled location.

### Share carefully

Share it only with authorized EDBB support staff through official support channels. The example PIN in this article cannot verify your service.

### Lost access?

If you cannot access the panel or find the welcome email, [contact EDBB support](/how-our-support-team-helps-you). Provide the VPS ID if you have it and explain that you cannot retrieve the PIN.
