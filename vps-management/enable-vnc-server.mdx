## Enable VNC for Console Access

VNC gives you a screen-and-keyboard connection to the VPS even when SSH or RDP is unavailable. Open your VPS in the [Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php). The controls are in the left-hand quick-action column.

## What You’ll Need

Use your signed-in panel session and a running VPS. Enable VNC while you need interactive console access, then disable it afterward. You can use **Open Console** in your browser or a desktop VNC viewer.

## Step 1 – Toggle VNC

Open the arrow beside **VNC Off** and select **Enable VNC**.

<Frame caption="Open the dropdown and choose Enable VNC.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/vnc-enable.png" alt="VNC Off dropdown with Enable VNC action" width="173" />
  </div>
</Frame>

Wait for **VNC On** and an enabled **Open Console** button. The panel also shows connection details for desktop viewers. Keep those details private.

<Frame caption="VNC On means console access is enabled; Open Console is now available.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/vnc-on.png" alt="Green VNC On control and enabled Open Console button" width="168" />
  </div>
</Frame>

## Step 2 – Open the Console

Click **Open Console**. A new browser tab opens the VPS display through your signed-in panel session. Click inside the display to send keyboard input. A Linux login prompt still requires your operating-system credentials.

<Frame caption="Example: a working browser console showing the Debian login prompt.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/vnc-console.png" alt="Browser VNC display showing Debian GNU Linux 13 and its login prompt" />
  </div>
</Frame>

The side handle opens the console toolbar, where you can find extra keys, clipboard, full-screen, settings and disconnect controls.

## Step 3 – Disable VNC When Finished

Return to the panel, open the arrow beside **VNC On**, and select **Disable VNC**.

<Frame caption="Choose Disable VNC when your console work is finished.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/vnc-disable.png" alt="VNC On dropdown with Disable VNC action" width="173" />
  </div>
</Frame>

Check that **VNC Off** is shown and **Open Console** is disabled. Use SSH or RDP for normal access afterward.

## Desktop VNC Client Instructions

1. Enable VNC in the panel.
2. Copy the **VNC Hostname**, including its port, into your VNC viewer.
3. Use the VNC password shown in the panel. This is separate from your operating-system login.
4. Connect, complete your console work, then disable VNC in the panel.

For connection problems, see [RealVNC disconnects](/misc/realvnc-disconnects). For boot media, see [Mount an ISO](/vps-management/mount-iso-image).
