## Shared responsibilities

EDBB supports infrastructure and service issues within the [support scope](/how-our-support-team-helps-you). You administer the guest operating system and applications, restrict access, apply security updates and maintain [independent backups](/backups).

Do not assume that a hosting location's certification establishes compliance for your application. If you have procurement or regulatory requirements, ask [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) for current evidence covering the specific entity, facility and service before relying on a claim.
