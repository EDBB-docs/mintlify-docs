Report suspected abuse involving an EDBB IP to [abuse@edbb.com](mailto:abuse@edbb.com).

Include the affected IP address, the activity, timestamps with timezone, and relevant logs or URLs. Explain how the evidence relates to the reported address. Do not include passwords, private keys or unnecessary personal data.

EDBB reviews reports under its [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy). For ordinary billing or service help, contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com).

If EDBB sent you an abuse notice, follow [responding to an abuse complaint](/misc/responding-to-abuse-complaints) and reply in that conversation with your investigation and remediation.
