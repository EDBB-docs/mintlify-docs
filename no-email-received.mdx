<span id="we-can-not-send-emails-again" />

If you cannot find a welcome email, check the service and payment status before changing anything on the VPS.

1. Check spam, junk and filtered folders for the address used on your EDBB account.
2. Sign in to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php) and confirm the invoice is paid and the service exists.
3. Open the service and review its **Dashboard**, IP details and **Installation** status. An installation still in progress is not ready for SSH or RDP.
4. If the email or access details remain unavailable, contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) from your account email with the order/invoice number. Do not send passwords.

<Warning>
  Do not reinstall merely to locate a missing email if the VPS contains data you need. Reinstallation erases the existing system. Use the [Linux](/vps-management/reset-root-password) or [Windows](/vps-management/reset-windows-password) recovery guide when appropriate.
</Warning>

A deliberate [reinstall](/vps-management/reinstall-vps) uses the password or SSH key you provide and does not send another welcome email.
