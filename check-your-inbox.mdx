After your payment is confirmed and the VPS is installed, we send a welcome email to the address registered to your EDBB account. Keep this email somewhere secure.

## What the email contains

- Your VPS's primary IPv4 address.
- The username and initial password for the operating system.
- Operating system and service details.
- A link to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php).

<span id="security-tip" />

Store the password in a password manager. Do not post the welcome email or its credentials in a public support request.

<span id="didnt-receive-the-email" />

## If the email is missing

Check spam and junk folders and confirm that your account email address is correct. Then follow [No welcome email? Get access to your VPS](/no-email-received). We cannot resend the original credentials, but you can choose a new password.

A later reinstall does not send another welcome email. Use the password or SSH key you chose during installation.
