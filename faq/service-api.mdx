---
title: "Does EDBB offer an API?"
sidebarTitle: "API availability"
description: "EDBB has no customer or order API. Use the EDBB control panel for service management and SSH or Cloud-Init for supported automation inside your VPS."
---

EDBB does not currently offer a customer management API or an order API. Use the [EDBB Control Panel](/vps-management/vps-control-panel) for service actions.

You can automate tasks inside your own operating system through SSH, [Cloud-Init](/vps-management/cloud-init-vps-installation) or configuration-management software. Guest automation does not provide an EDBB provisioning or billing API.

EDBB account, billing and service actions are managed through the control panel.
