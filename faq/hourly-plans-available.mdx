EDBB does not offer hourly VPS billing. Services use the billing period shown on the order and invoice; powering off the VPS does not stop its subscription charges.

Use [EDBB plans](https://www.edbb.com/) to check current resources and billing choices. The same EDBB VPS plan has the same price across locations. An annual billing cycle provides 12 months of service for the price of 11. EDBB does not offer other discounts, a volume-business programme, a customer API or an order API.

You can automate tasks **inside your VPS** through SSH, Cloud-Init or configuration-management tools. That does not change billing or provide an EDBB service API.

See [cancel a VPS](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) when a service is no longer needed, and the separate [refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy) for eligible new purchases.
