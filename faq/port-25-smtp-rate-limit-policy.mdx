## Is Port 25 available?

Yes. EDBB permits SMTP traffic on TCP Port 25, subject to the following controls:

| Direction | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbound TCP Port 25** | Unrestricted |
| **Outbound TCP Port 25** | Open, with a default rate limit on VPS instances created on or after **February 24, 2025** |

The outbound rate limit is **8 packets per second (pps)**, with an **initial burst of up to 16 packets**.

<Note>
  The effective date is based on the creation date of the individual VPS, not
  the creation date of your customer account. VPS instances created before February
  24, 2025 are not subject to this automatic default rate limit. Our
  Acceptable Use Policy and anti-abuse controls apply to every VPS.
</Note>

This is a network packet rate limit, not an allowance for a specific number of email messages.

## Why outbound Port 25 is rate-limited

The rate limit helps EDBB:

- Protect the reputation of its IP address ranges
- Reduce unsolicited email and compromised-server abuse
- Preserve reliable email delivery for legitimate users
- Respond quickly to abuse while keeping Port 25 available for normal use

## Can the outbound rate limit be removed?

The outbound Port 25 rate limit is **not a standard paid add-on**, and removal is not generally available.

EDBB may, at its sole discretion, approve an exception for an established and verifiable business operating a legitimate, consent-based email service. Contact [EDBB Support](mailto:support@edbb.com) before beginning such activity.

As part of the review, we may request:

- The legal business name and responsible contact person
- A public website and verifiable business presence
- The sending domains and a detailed description of the use case
- Expected sending and recipient volumes
- An explanation of how recipients provide consent
- Unsubscribe, suppression, bounce, and complaint-handling procedures
- A working abuse contact
- Correct rDNS, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configuration
- Confirmation that the VPS is not operating as an open relay

Any approval applies only to the **specific VPS reviewed**. It is not account-wide and may be revoked at any time.

<Warning>
  Payment, account credit, identity verification, supporting documents, or the
  submission of a request does not guarantee approval. Exceptions are not
  approved for cold email, purchased or scraped recipient lists, unsolicited
  bulk email, or other activity that violates our
  [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy).
</Warning>

## What happens if spam is reported?

EDBB may immediately block **outbound TCP Port 25 for the entire VPS** if a credible complaint or other evidence indicates that unsolicited email was sent from any IP address assigned to that VPS.

The block applies to **all IPv4 and IPv6 addresses assigned to the VPS**, even if the complaint concerns only one IP address. Inbound TCP Port 25 remains unaffected.

A single substantiated complaint may be sufficient. You must promptly:

1. Investigate and stop the reported activity
2. Secure any compromised accounts, credentials, applications, or software
3. Address the cause of the incident
4. Respond to EDBB with the remediation taken

The outbound Port 25 block may remain in place indefinitely if the incident is not resolved satisfactorily. Repeated complaints, deliberate unsolicited mailing, or failure to cooperate may result in additional service restrictions, suspension, or account termination without refund.

See our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy) and [Responding to an abuse complaint](/misc/responding-to-abuse-complaints) for further information.

## Recommendation for VPN operators

We recommend blocking outbound TCP port 25 in your VPN software or firewall unless your service has a legitimate requirement for direct SMTP delivery. Cover both traffic originating on the VPS and traffic forwarded from VPN subscribers, for IPv4 and IPv6 where enabled.

The default platform rate limit is not a complete outbound block and does not replace your own abuse controls. A substantiated spam complaint can trigger an outbound port 25 block for the entire VPS, including every assigned IP address, as described above.

## Need higher email capacity?

For high-volume transactional or marketing email, we recommend using a specialized, authenticated email delivery service with established bounce, suppression, and complaint management.
