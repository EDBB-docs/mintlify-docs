## Why It Happens

The current EDBB payment page shows the payment address, currency, network, amount and expiry timer. Network congestion or a wallet or exchange withdrawal delay may cause a payment to arrive after that window.

A transaction broadcast before expiry is not necessarily confirmed or credited before expiry. An expired payment session and an unpaid invoice need review; do not assume that the payment has been lost or refunded.

## What to Do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the payment and invoice status">
    Save the message displayed by the payment page and check the EDBB invoice. Do not send a second payment while the first is unresolved, and do not reuse an expired payment address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the details">
    Collect the transaction hash (TXID), currency, network, amount, approximate sending time, receiving address and invoice or payment reference, if available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact EDBB support">
    Send these details to [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) or use the portal messenger. Our team will review the transaction and invoice records and explain the available next step. Manual credit, recovery or a refund cannot be promised before that review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For future payments, follow the timer and instructions on the current invoice page. Check that your wallet or exchange can send the exact currency on the exact network shown. See [crypto confirmations](/faq/billing-lifecycle/crypto-confirmations).
