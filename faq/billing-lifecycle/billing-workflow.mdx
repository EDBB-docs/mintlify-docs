Your VPS renews unless you cancel it. Renewal invoices and automatic payment arrangements are separate: check that each invoice is paid even if you have a saved payment method.

<span id="simplified-vps-billing-workflow" />

## Non-payment timeline

Day 0 is the renewal invoice due date. The following schedule applies to **non-payment**, not a cancellation you request yourself.

| Relative to due date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 7 days before | Renewal pro-forma invoice created; available account credit is applied by default |
| 3 days before | Automatic attempt to charge a saved credit card |
| 2 days before | Unpaid reminder |
| Due date | Payment due |
| 1, 2 and 3 days after | Overdue reminders |
| 7 days after | VPS suspended and powered down |
| 14 days after | Service terminated |
| 20 days after | Data permanently deleted, six days after termination; renewal invoice cancelled |

- **Already terminated:** contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) promptly to request recovery or reactivation before permanent deletion.

## Invoice cancellation is not always data deletion

In the automated non-payment workflow, invoice cancellation accompanies final deletion. If **you requested immediate or end-of-term cancellation**, the renewal invoice can be cancelled earlier. On a combined invoice, only the cancelled VPS's charges are removed.

![Example invoice row showing Cancelled status](/assets/invoice-status-cancelled-cropped.webp)
</Frame>

The screenshot illustrates a cancelled **invoice**, not confirmation that VPS data has been deleted. The displayed amount is an example.

The data-retention clock starts at **actual service termination**, not invoice cancellation. See [cancel a VPS](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) and [data retention](/faq/billing-lifecycle/data-retention-period).

## Avoid an unwanted renewal

Cancel before the next renewal invoice is generated if you no longer need the VPS. Turning it off does not cancel billing. Manage a PayPal subscription separately if one exists, and check the invoice status after any change to automatic payment arrangements.

For an eligible mistaken payment, see [accidental renewal](/faq/billing-lifecycle/accidental-renewal). EDBB plans use EDBB's own prices and allowances, with the same VPS plan price across locations.

## Annual billing

An annual cycle provides **12 months of service for the price of 11**. Review the annual total in EDBB checkout. This is the confirmed billing-cycle offer; no separate volume-discount programme is available.
