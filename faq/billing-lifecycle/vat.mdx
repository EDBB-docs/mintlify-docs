EDBB is operated by **ED Backbone & Co**, a legal partnership in Cyprus. Prices on the EDBB website are shown in EUR excluding VAT; checkout determines the tax on your invoice from your billing details and applicable rules.

Keep your billing country, address and business VAT ID accurate before ordering. A VAT ID must be validated before you rely on any business tax treatment. Do not assume every EU business qualifies for the same treatment, particularly a business in the supplier's own country.

If the tax or billing details appear incorrect, contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) with the invoice number before payment. Do not change your country to obtain a different tax result.

See [EDBB legal information](https://www.edbb.com/legal) and [download an invoice](/faq/billing-lifecycle/find-and-download-invoices). Tax treatment and any deduction available to your business depend on your circumstances; your adviser can assess those details.
