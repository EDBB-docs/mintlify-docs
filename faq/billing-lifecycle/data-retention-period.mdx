<span id="vps-retention-timeline" />

VPS data is retained for **six days after service termination**, then permanently deleted. The retention period is not a routine backup service and does not guarantee continued access to a stopped VPS.

| Situation | Retention starts |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate cancellation | When the service terminates immediately |
| End-of-term cancellation | When the service terminates on its due date |
| Termination for non-payment | At termination, normally 14 days after the renewal due date |

Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) promptly to request recovery or reactivation before deletion. After permanent deletion, recovery is not possible. Back up data **before** cancelling.

An invoice being cancelled or adjusted is a billing action; it does not by itself prove that VPS data has been deleted. See [cancellation](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service) and the [non-payment workflow](/faq/billing-lifecycle/billing-workflow).

<span id="qcow2-export-availability" />

## Paid qcow2 exports

A separately requested [qcow2 disk export](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/qcow2-export) costs **EUR 25** and is supplied through a password-protected download link available for **30 days**. Download and store your own copy before the file is removed. This download period is separate from the six-day VPS retention period.
