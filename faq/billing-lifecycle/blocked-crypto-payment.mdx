## Why Payments Are Blocked

A payment marked **blocked**, **held** or **under review** is different from a transaction that is simply awaiting blockchain confirmations. The exact message on the current EDBB payment page determines which follow-up is needed.

Do not assume that every delay is a compliance block, that funds have reached EDBB, or that completing a questionnaire guarantees release. Blockchain confirmation alone does not establish that an EDBB invoice has been credited.

## What to Do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save the status message">
    Record the exact message shown on the payment page and check whether the EDBB invoice is marked paid. Do not make another payment while the first is unresolved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the transaction details">
    Have the invoice or payment reference, transaction hash (TXID), currency, network, receiving address, amount and approximate sending time ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact EDBB support">
    Use the portal messenger or email [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com). Our team will review the available records and provide the applicable next steps. We cannot promise automatic release, invoice credit, recovery or a refund before review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If additional verification is needed, follow the private instructions provided through EDBB's verified support channels. Never share a seed phrase, private key or wallet password. Do not post identity documents in public channels.

Use the provider and instructions shown on your actual payment page. Old Cryptomus questionnaires, Telegram contacts and other providers' workflows do not apply automatically to current EDBB payments.

If the transaction is still waiting for confirmations, see [crypto confirmations](/faq/billing-lifecycle/crypto-confirmations). If it arrived after expiry, see [late crypto payments](/faq/billing-lifecycle/late-crypto-payment).
