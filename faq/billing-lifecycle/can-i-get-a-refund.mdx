<span id="eligibility-checklist" />

Eligible VPS purchases may qualify for a refund if EDBB receives the complete request **within 24 hours of purchase**. Only one refund per customer is available in any 12-month period.

Refunds are not automatic. Completed location changes, added Pool Traffic, plan/resource upgrades, revoked cancellation requests and service closure for policy violations affect eligibility. A free primary IPv4 change within the original window does not itself remove refund eligibility.

Read the full [cancellation and refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy). It contains the eligibility conditions, immediate-cancellation requirement and request template. Send the IP address and Support PIN to support before the deadline.

Outside that policy, cancelling does not create a general right to a refund or account credit. A mistaken renewal has a separate, limited [credit-conversion review](/faq/billing-lifecycle/accidental-renewal).
