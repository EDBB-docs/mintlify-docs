Add a contact to receive invoices and billing reminders at an additional email address.

## Open Contacts/Sub-Accounts

Sign in to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php), open your account menu in the top-right corner and choose **Contacts/Sub-Accounts**. Select **Add New Contact** in **Choose Contact**, then click **Go** if needed. If no contacts exist, the new-contact form opens directly.

## Enter the recipient and select Invoice Emails

Enter the recipient's name, company and email address, plus any other fields the form requires. Under **Email Preferences**, select **Invoice Emails - Invoices & Billing Reminders**.

Enable other email categories only if the recipient should receive them. **Activate Sub-Account** is a separate option for client-area access; receiving invoice emails does not require enabling it.

## Save and review

Click **Save Changes**. Reopen the contact using **Choose Contact** and verify the email address and **Invoice Emails** selection. Future invoice notifications can then be sent to this contact as well as the primary account address. Adding a contact does not resend earlier invoices.

To update the recipient, select the existing contact, edit its details and save. See [Find and download invoices](/faq/billing-lifecycle/find-and-download-invoices) for existing documents.
