## Authorization (Reservation)

An authorization reserves an amount on your card without transferring it to EDBB. Your banking app may display it as a pending transaction, reservation or card entry.

A checkout session can fail after an authorization was created, for example if the connection is interrupted or a VPN changes your IP address. In that case, you may see a reservation even though no order or invoice was created.

- An uncaptured reservation is not money received by EDBB, so we cannot refund it.
- The card issuer releases an uncaptured reservation automatically. The timing depends on the issuer; we cannot guarantee a particular release date.
- Your card issuer can explain how the entry is displayed and when a confirmed reservation expires.

## Captured Payment

A captured payment is a completed charge. If it has been captured but the order or invoice is missing or still unpaid, contact EDBB so our team can reconcile the payment.

An approved refund of a captured payment is separate from the release of an authorization. The time until a refund appears depends on your payment provider.

## If the payment status is unclear

<Warning>
  A missing order, an unpaid invoice or the wording in a banking app does not by itself establish whether a payment was captured. Do not make another payment until the first attempt has been clarified.
</Warning>

Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) or use the portal messenger. Include:

- Your EDBB account email address, or the email used at checkout.
- The amount, currency and approximate date and time of the attempt.
- The order or invoice reference, **if one exists**.
- The status shown by your bank, such as pending or completed. If you share a screenshot, hide unrelated transactions and sensitive card information.

You do **not** need a VPS IP address or Support PIN if no VPS was provisioned. Never send a full card number, CVC or account password.

Our team will check the available payment records to distinguish an authorization, a captured payment or an unresolved attempt. If capture is confirmed, we will review why the payment was not assigned correctly and explain the next step.
