Cancel your VPS from its EDBB Control Panel record using **Request Cancellation**. Choose immediate cancellation or cancellation at the end of the paid billing period.

<Warning>
  Back up data before requesting cancellation. Immediate termination stops access to the VPS. Six days after actual termination, its data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
</Warning>

## Submit the request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the control panel">
    Open the <a href="https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php" target="_blank">
     EDBB Control Panel</a>

    and go to <strong>Services → My Products</strong>
     .
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the VPS record">
    Click the service you want to cancel to view its management dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Request Cancellation">
    In the right-hand panel, choose <strong>Request Cancellation</strong>
     button. Select whether the VPS should stop <strong>immediately</strong>
     or <strong>at the end of the billing period</strong>
     .
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the request">
    Submit the form. The Control Panel updates the service status and sends an email confirming your cancellation and the chosen cancellation option (immediate/expiry)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Choose the timing deliberately. This example selects End of Billing Period; Request Cancellation submits the form.">

    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/cancellation-form.png" alt="Cancellation reason field timing selector set to End of Billing Period and submit button" />

The form initially selects **Immediate**. Review **Cancellation Type** before submitting. The **Cancel** link leaves the form without requesting service cancellation.

## Cancellation timing

| Choice | Service stops | Permanent deletion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate | At immediate termination | Six days after termination |
| End of billing period | On the service due date | Six days after that termination date |

Cancellation removes this VPS's renewal line items from outstanding pro-forma invoices. An invoice covering only this service is cancelled; other services on a combined invoice remain payable. This adjustment does not itself delete data.

Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) promptly if you need recovery or reactivation before deletion. Do not rely on the retention period instead of making your own backups.

Unassigned Traffic Pool balance belongs to your account and does not expire when a VPS ends. Traffic already assigned to a VPS is separate; see [Traffic Pool](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool).

Paid monthly charges are not refunded simply because you stop a service early. Eligible new purchases are handled under the [24-hour refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy). See the separate [non-payment timeline](/faq/billing-lifecycle/billing-workflow).
