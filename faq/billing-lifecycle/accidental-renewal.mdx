<span id="turn-an-accidental-renewal-into-credit" />

If a VPS renews accidentally, you can ask support to convert the payment to account credit. This is a reviewable request, not a general entitlement to a refund.

<span id="eligibility-checklist" />

## Eligibility

- Submit the request **before the service due date**.
- The payment must have been made **within 24 hours of your request**.
- Cancel the affected service and include its invoice number, IP address and your reason for the request.

EDBB reviews the request and may decline it. If approved, the payment becomes account credit for future services; it is not returned as a direct payment.

<span id="what-to-do" />

## Request a review

1. Back up any data you need, then [cancel the service](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancel-a-service).
2. Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) with the invoice number, service/IP details and explanation.
3. Check the support response and account balance. Do not assume conversion has completed until it is confirmed.

New VPS purchases have a separate [24-hour refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy).
