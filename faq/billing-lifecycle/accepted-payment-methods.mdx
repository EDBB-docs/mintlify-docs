<span id="payment-gateways-at-a-glance" />

EDBB accepts the following payment methods. Use the options shown on your invoice; provider availability and eligibility can vary by account and payment location.

<span id="digital-wallets" />

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Credit cards | One-off or recurring payments processed by Stripe |
| PayPal | One-off checkout or a separately established recurring subscription |
| Cryptocurrencies | Payments processed by Cryptomus; use the exact currency and network shown for the invoice |
| Apple Pay, Google Pay and Alipay | Supported wallet methods where available in checkout |
| SEPA and ACH Direct Debit | Invitation only, after mandate approval; settlement is not immediate |
| SEPA Credit Transfer or SWIFT | Manually reconciled bank transfers; contact support for EDBB-specific instructions |

<span id="cards" />

## Cards and recurring payments

Supported card networks include American Express, China UnionPay, Discover/Diners, JCB, Mastercard and Visa. Cards issued by Russian banks are not supported by the payment networks used for this service.

Renewal invoices are created seven days before the due date. A saved-card payment attempt occurs three days before the due date. Account credit is applied by default when a renewal invoice is issued. A PayPal subscription is managed separately in PayPal. See [billing and renewals](/faq/billing-lifecycle/billing-workflow).

<span id="cryptocurrencies" />

## Cryptocurrency

Choose the coin and network on the invoice payment page. Send the specified amount to the address for that payment; do not reuse an old address or select a different network. The processor must confirm payment before provisioning begins. See [crypto confirmations](/faq/billing-lifecycle/crypto-confirmations).

EDBB adds no cryptocurrency surcharge. Network fees and wallet or exchange fees are separate.

## Bank transfers

Bank transfers primarily fund account-credit top-ups of **at least EUR 250**. Emergency payment of an invoice by bank transfer requires prior agreement with support. Transfers are received, identified and assigned manually, so they are unsuitable for urgent renewals or immediate provisioning.

[Download EDBB EUR bank details (PDF)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EDBB-docs/mintlify-docs/main/assets/edbb-eur-bank-details.pdf)

The PDF contains the **ED BACKBONE & CO** EUR account details at **iBanFirst**. Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) to confirm the correct payment reference and arrange any invoice payment by bank transfer. Use these EDBB details for EDBB payments.

<Warning>
  Sending a transfer does not mark an invoice as paid or stop suspension and deletion deadlines. Check that payment has been assigned before relying on the funds. Use an automated method for a time-sensitive invoice.
</Warning>
