## Find your invoices

Open **Billing → My Invoices** in the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php).

## Find the document

Use the search box above the table, or the **Status** choices on the left. Click a column heading to sort by invoice number, invoice date, due date, total or status. Click the invoice row to open it.

<Frame caption="Filter the list by Paid, Unpaid, Cancelled or Refunded. Counts depend on your account.">

## Download the PDF

On the invoice page, scroll to the bottom and click **Download**. The adjacent **Print** action opens the browser print flow.

<Frame caption="Print and Download are at the bottom of the opened invoice.">

    <img src="/assets/panel-workflows/invoice-download.png" alt="Print and Download buttons on an invoice" width="280" />
  </div>
</Frame>

Keep downloaded invoices with your accounting records. For billing dates and renewals, see [How VPS billing works](/faq/billing-lifecycle/billing-workflow).
