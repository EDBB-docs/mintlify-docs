The Traffic Pool is a shared account reserve. Assign traffic from it to a VPS when the monthly allowance is insufficient. Every newly purchased VPS adds **100 GB starter credit** to your pool.

## Which traffic expires?

| Traffic | Where it is held | At the VPS monthly refill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly Plan Traffic | Included with that EDBB VPS plan | Usage resets; unused allowance does not carry forward |
| Unassigned Pool Traffic | Shared account pool | Does not expire |
| Assigned Pool Traffic | Added to one VPS's current allowance | Any unused remainder expires |
| 100 GB starter credit | Shared account pool | Does not expire while unassigned |

Your **EDBB plan** sets the included monthly allowance. Check the Traffic tab for your VPS's current balance and refill date.

<span id="how-to-get-started" />

## Buy and assign traffic

Open the VPS in the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php) and select the **Traffic** tab. Use **Top up** or the [EDBB traffic purchase page](https://my.edbb.com/cart.php?gid=214). Confirm the amount and price in checkout.

After payment is confirmed, assign traffic using **Manual Hydration**: **+100 GB**, **+500 GB**, **+1 TB** or **+10 TB**, then **Assign Traffic**. The amount is deducted from the account pool and added to this VPS's current allowance.

  Check the monthly refill date before assigning a large amount. Assigned traffic expires at that refill even if unused. Purchasing alone does not assign traffic to a VPS.

## Auto-Hydration

Enable Auto-Hydration separately for each VPS and select **100 GB**, **200 GB**, **300 GB** or **500 GB**. At **99% of the current allowance**, the selected amount is assigned from the pool if enough balance is available.

The current allowance includes Plan Traffic and traffic already assigned during the cycle. Auto-Hydration uses existing pool balance; it never purchases traffic automatically.

## Read the logs

Use **Hydration log** and **Pool log** to distinguish assignment to a VPS from actual network usage. A pool deduction means traffic was assigned, not that it was already consumed.

This fictional example uses GB consistently and starts with a zero pool balance. Newest transactions are shown first.

| Event | Pool change | New pool balance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assign traffic to demo-vps-b | -100 GB | 500 GB |
| Starter credit for demo-vps-b | +100 GB | 600 GB |
| Assign traffic to demo-vps-a | -500 GB | 500 GB |
| Purchase traffic | +1,000 GB | 1,000 GB |

## Low-balance notifications

After a top-up, the resulting pool balance becomes the reference for the low-balance notice. When **80% or more has been assigned**, an email indicates that **20% or less remains**. Fixed assignment increments can cross that threshold and leave less than 20%, including zero.

A low pool balance does not stop a VPS that still has traffic available. It prevents further assignments when the selected increment cannot be funded. See [traffic statistics](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-statistics) and [traffic limits](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/exceeding-monthly-included-traffic).
