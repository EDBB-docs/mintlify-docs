## Understand the allowance

**Current allowance = monthly Plan Traffic + Pool Traffic assigned during this cycle.**

**Remaining traffic = current allowance − traffic used this cycle.**

Traffic usage counts both **incoming and outgoing** data measured on the VPS Ethernet interfaces. Read the units displayed by the panel; GB measures transferred data, not connection speed.

In this closeup, **1 GB / 20,580 GB** means approximately **20,579 GB remains**. The percentage can round to **0%** when usage is small. The **1,400 GB pool balance** is separate: it becomes part of a VPS allowance only after assignment.

## Compare daily and cumulative usage

In the illustrative overview, daily usage peaks at **300 GB on September 25**. That day's transfer also creates the steepest increase in the cumulative line. The seven sample days total **1,240 GB**.

Use the daily chart to locate a spike, then compare that date with backups, downloads, application jobs and server logs. The cumulative chart helps you see how quickly the total is growing. For unexpected usage, follow [Investigate network usage](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/investigate-network-usage).

## Monthly refill

The VPS's monthly service cycle determines its refill, not the calendar month. **Refill in … day(s)** shows the countdown. At refill, usage resets and the plan allowance is replenished. Unused Plan Traffic and Pool Traffic already assigned to the VPS expire; unassigned pool balance does not expire.

<span id="manage-traffic-pool-hydration" />

## Distinguish usage from assignments

**Hydration log** records traffic assigned to a VPS; **Pool log** shows pool activity. A pool deduction does not mean that amount has already crossed the network. See [Traffic Pool and Auto-Hydration](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool) for manual 100 GB assignments and Auto-Hydration ON/OFF instructions.

<span id="what-happens-at-100%-usage" />

## Notifications and rate limiting

| Threshold | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 80% of current allowance | Informational email |
| 90% | Warning, suppressed if Auto-Hydration is enabled and the pool can cover its next increment |
| 99% | Auto-Hydration assigns the selected 100, 200, 300 or 500 GB if the balance is sufficient |
| Approximately 105% | About 64 kbit/s rate limit and an action-required notice |

There is no automatic overage charge. To restore speed, assign pool traffic, complete a qualifying plan upgrade or wait for the monthly refill. See [traffic limits](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/exceeding-monthly-included-traffic).
