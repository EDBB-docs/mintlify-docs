EDBB uses the shared infrastructure protection provided by the EDIS Global resource pool. Protection focuses on limiting the effect of attacks on the network and other customers; EDBB does not provide service-specific DDoS scrubbing or bespoke mitigation.

## During an attack

The infrastructure detects abnormal traffic and may filter or redirect it. If necessary, the attacked IP can be null-routed to protect the rest of the network. Null-routing makes the affected service unreachable; it is not a guarantee that your application stays online.

Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) with the affected IP, timestamps, symptoms and relevant traffic observations. Check the legitimate shared [infrastructure status service](https://status.edis.global/).

For frequently targeted applications, review [high-risk workloads](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/dos-attacks/high-risk-workloads) and arrange suitable mitigation before deployment. Prohibited attack activity is covered by the [AUP](/acceptable-use-policy).
