**Traffic** is the amount of data transferred. **Bandwidth** describes how much data a connection can carry per second.

| Term | Typical units | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traffic | GB or TB | The total data your VPS sends and receives during its monthly traffic cycle. |
| Bandwidth or connection speed | Mbit/s or Gbit/s | How quickly a file can be transferred at a particular moment. |

## What counts towards your allowance?

EDBB counts **incoming plus outgoing traffic**. Uploads, downloads, backups and application traffic all contribute to the total.

Each VPS plan includes a monthly traffic allowance. Your account also has a separate **Traffic Pool**, from which you can assign more traffic to a VPS. The pool and the VPS allowance are not the same balance. See [Traffic Pool](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool).

## Does a faster connection include more traffic?

No. Connection speed and traffic allowance are separate. A fast transfer uses the same amount of traffic as a slow transfer of the same data.

An advertised infrastructure uplink speed is not guaranteed throughput for one VPS. Actual speed depends on the plan, destination, route and current load.

Open the VPS's **Traffic** tab to check [usage and the next refill date](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-statistics). If its allowance runs out, follow [restore network speed](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/exceeding-monthly-included-traffic).
