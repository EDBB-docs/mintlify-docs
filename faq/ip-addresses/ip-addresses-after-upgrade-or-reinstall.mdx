## What Happens to Your IPs

<Info>
  Reinstalling the operating system or changing your VPS plan does <strong>not</strong> rotate the assigned IPv4 or IPv6. The addresses stay linked to the service for as long as it remains active.
</Info>

---

### The Exceptions

- Cancelling or letting the VPS expire releases the IPs back into EDBB’s pool.
- Ordering a free (first 24 hours) or paid IP change assigns a fresh address.
- Adding secondary IPs appends new addresses but leaves the primary untouched.

A location replacement creates a new VPS with new IP and login details; see [location changes](/faq/server-locations/change-server-location). IP continuity is separate from backups and upgrade activation.
