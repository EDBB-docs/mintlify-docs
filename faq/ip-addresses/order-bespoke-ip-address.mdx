## Bespoke Address Option

<Info>
  Bespoke selection lets you discuss available provider-assigned addresses with support. It cannot guarantee a third-party location label, reputation or reachability. EDBB sources the addresses from curated pools so you can hand-pick addresses available for your service.
</Info>

<span id="pricing-snapshot" />

## Price and availability

Confirm the current EDBB price, recurring charge and available quantity in the order summary or with [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) before payment. Do not use EDIS Global prices as an EDBB quote.

------|-------|
| One-time selection fee per IPv4 | **€10** |
| Monthly charge per IPv4 | **€5** |

---

## How to Request a Bespoke IPv4

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact EDBB Support">
    From the EDBB Control Panel, reach out via the messenger or by email. Include your VPS identifier (primary IPv4 or KVM ID) and how many bespoke addresses you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Support will reply with the curated list of available addresses for your location. Pick the exact IPv4s you want, confirm the selection, and pay the invoice they send. The chosen addresses appear under the **Network** tab as soon as the order is fulfilled.

Finish by configuring each IPv4 manually within your operating system.

[Prefer automated assignment?](/faq/ip-addresses/order-additional-ip-address)

Use the [VNC console](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server) before changing network settings. Preserve existing addresses and data. Reinstalling erases the VPS and is not necessary just to add a guest network address.
