If you would like to choose an IPv4 address from the available pool at your VPS's location, contact support before ordering.

<span id="pricing-snapshot" />

<span id="bespoke-address-option" />

## Price and availability

Support will confirm which addresses are available, any one-time selection fee and the recurring charge. Review the quote before you pay.

Choosing an address does not guarantee its reputation, its location in third-party databases or its reachability from a particular network. See [IP geolocation](/faq/ip-addresses/geolocation-geoip-databases) and [country reachability](/faq/ip-addresses/country-reachability).

<span id="how-to-request-a-bespoke-ipv4" />

## Request an address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Email [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) or use the website messenger. Include your VPS's primary IPv4 address or service ID, how many addresses you need, and what you are looking for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the available choices and price">
    Support will explain the available options. Confirm your selection and the charges before paying the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the assigned address">
    Once the order is completed, find the address and network settings in your VPS's **Network** tab. Add the address to the operating system while keeping the existing primary address and gateway.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Open the [VNC console](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server) before changing networking. On Linux, follow [configure an additional IPv4 address](/faq/ip-addresses/configure-additional-ip-linux). You do not need to reinstall the VPS to add an address.

If you do not need to choose a particular address, use [Additional IPs in Upgrade Components](/faq/ip-addresses/order-additional-ip-address).
