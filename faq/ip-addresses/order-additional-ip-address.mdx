You can order additional provider-assigned IPv4 addresses for your VPS. Available addresses come from the pool at your server's location.

<span id="pricing-snapshot" />

<span id="what%E2%80%99s-included" />

## Price and availability

The **Upgrades** tab shows the available quantity and current price for your service. Check the recurring charge and total before paying. If the option is unavailable, ask [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) about availability.

<span id="how-to-place-the-order" />

<span id="option-b%3A-order-via-upgrade-components" />

## Order in the control panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Upgrade Components">
    Sign in to the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php), open **Services**, select your VPS, then choose **Upgrades → Upgrade Components**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Additional IPs">
    Select the quantity you need within the limit shown for your VPS. Review the new total and complete checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the assigned addresses">
    After payment is processed, open the VPS's **Network** tab. Copy the new IPv4 addresses and their network settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the addresses on your server">
    Add the addresses to your operating system. They are not ready for use merely because they appear in the panel. Follow [configure an additional Linux IPv4 address](/faq/ip-addresses/configure-additional-ip-linux), or add them to the existing network adapter's advanced TCP/IP settings on Windows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep your existing primary address and gateway. Reinstalling is not necessary to add an IP address and would erase the VPS disk. Open the [VNC console](/vps-management/enable-vnc-server) before changing network settings.
</Note>

<span id="option-a%3A-chat-with-support" />

## Ask support to help with the order

Contact [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) or use the website messenger. Include your VPS's primary IP address or service ID and the number of additional addresses you need. Support will confirm availability and the charges before you pay.

To ask about choosing a particular address, see [bespoke IPv4 addresses](/faq/ip-addresses/order-bespoke-ip-address).
