Test connectivity from the networks your users actually use before choosing a VPS location. Geographic distance alone does not determine latency or routing.

Open the relevant location on the [EDBB website](https://www.edbb.com/). Its **Looking Glass**, test addresses and download links provide the available network-test destinations for that location. Use the displayed current destination instead of an address copied from another location.

## Useful tests

- Ping for round-trip latency, where the target permits ICMP.
- Traceroute or MTR for the route and end-to-end packet loss.
- A download test for a sample of throughput along that particular path.

Repeat at relevant times and compare actual application performance. Recorded speed-test results and host-uplink capacity are not a guarantee of per-VPS throughput or future performance. Your ISP, route, destination and workload all matter.

See [diagnose packet loss](/faq/ip-addresses/diagnose-packet-loss) and [country-specific reachability](/faq/ip-addresses/country-reachability).
