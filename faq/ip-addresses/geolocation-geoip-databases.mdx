Commercial GeoIP databases estimate an IP address's location. Their labels can differ from the VPS's physical hosting location and can change independently of your service.

## Sources are different

- Registry/WHOIS information records allocation and registration data. Its country field is not definitive proof of where a server runs.
- Commercial providers maintain their own location databases and correction processes.
- A geofeed provides provider-published location information in the [RFC 8805 format](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8805.html): prefix, country, region, city and postal code. It does not contain latitude/longitude columns.

## Report a mismatch

Identify the exact database or website displaying the incorrect result, the IP address, the displayed location and your ordered VPS location. Send the evidence to [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com), and use the database vendor's current official correction form where available.

EDBB cannot guarantee when a third party will accept or distribute an update. Changing a PTR record alone does not update every location database. See [IP location](/faq/ip-addresses/ip-location) and [correct geolocation](/faq/ip-addresses/correct-geolocation).
