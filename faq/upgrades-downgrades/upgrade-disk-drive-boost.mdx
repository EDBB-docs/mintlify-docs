Drive Boost increases the virtual disk capacity of your existing VPS. It does not reinstall the operating system or automatically enlarge its partitions and filesystems.

## Order additional storage

1. Open the VPS in the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php).
2. Open **Upgrades** and choose **Upgrade Now** under **Upgrade Components**.
3. Review the storage additions and EDBB prices offered for that service. Confirm the resulting total disk size, recurring charge and prorated invoice before payment.
4. Follow the activation instructions after payment and perform a full [power cycle](/vps-management/power-cycle-or-reboot-a-vps) when required to expose the new capacity.

Do not assume an EDIS Global storage tier, limit or price applies to EDBB. The current EDBB order summary is the source for commercial values. Ask [support@edbb.com](mailto:support@edbb.com) if the total or billing is unclear.

## Expand the guest filesystem

Make a backup first. Check the new virtual disk size, identify the partition/filesystem layout, then follow the appropriate [Linux](/advanced-setup-guides/resize-linux-disk) or [Windows](/advanced-setup-guides/extend-windows-disk) procedure. Do not delete another partition just to create adjacent free space.

Storage increases are not an in-place downgrade path. See the [downgrade policy](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/downgrade-policy) before committing to a larger allocation.
