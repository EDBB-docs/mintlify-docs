A plan upgrade adds the resources included in a larger plan while retaining your existing installation and IP address. If you only need individual components, see [Upgrade resources](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-resources).

## Open the plan upgrade

Select your VPS under **Services → My Services** in the [EDBB Control Panel](https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php). Click **Upgrade Plan** in the service card, or open **Upgrades** and choose **Upgrade Your Plan**.

## Choose a plan and billing cycle

Review the available plans under **New Configuration**. Compare their CPU, RAM, storage and traffic. Select a **Billing cycle**, then click **Choose Product** to see the quote.

## Review the prorated quote

The review page identifies the current and new plan and shows **Subtotal**, applicable tax and **Total Due Today**. The panel explains the remaining billing period used for the calculation: unused time on the current plan is credited against the new plan for the same period.

<Frame caption="Example quote from a test service. Use the amount displayed for your own order.">
  <div style={{ background: "#0b1830", padding: "24px", borderRadius: "12px", borderTop: "3px solid #14b8a6" }}>
    <img src="/assets/guide-batch/plan-review.png" alt="Plan upgrade quote showing the plan change, tax and amount due today" />
  </div>
</Frame>

Choose your payment method, continue and complete payment. Follow the activation instructions in the upgrade email.

<Warning>
Back up important data and schedule downtime. Perform a full **Power Cycle VPS** from the panel when instructed to activate CPU or RAM changes. A restart inside the operating system is not the same operation.
</Warning>

## Verify the new resources

- **IP address and installation:** a plan upgrade does not require a reinstall.
- **RAM and vCPU:** check the allocation after the required power cycle.
- **Disk:** a larger virtual disk does not automatically enlarge every partition or filesystem. Follow [Linux disk expansion](/advanced-setup-guides/resize-linux-disk) or [Windows disk expansion](/advanced-setup-guides/extend-windows-disk).
- **Traffic:** check the new allowance in the panel.

See [Restart and power cycle](/vps-management/power-cycle-or-reboot-a-vps) for the panel controls, or [Traffic Pool](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool) if you only need more data transfer.
