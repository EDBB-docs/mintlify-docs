## Upgrade a VPS Plan in Minutes

<Info>
  A plan upgrade adds the resources included in a larger plan without reinstalling your VPS or changing its IP address.
</Info>

## Why Upgrade

- Add CPU, memory, and disk space in one step.
- Boost the monthly traffic allowance tied to the plan.
- Keep your data intact; the upgrade happens in-place.

<Info>
  Upgrades are prorated. You pay only the price difference for the remaining days in your billing cycle.
</Info>

---

## Option 1 — Use the Quick Upgrade Card

If you already know which tier you need, click the upgrade shortcut on the service Control Panel.

![Quick upgrade button on the dashboard](/assets/Upgrade-plan-card.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the VPS dashboard">
    Sign in to the <a href="https://my.edbb.com/clientarea.php" target="_blank">EDBB Control Panel</a> and choose the VPS you want to upgrade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Upgrade">
    Click the green <strong>Upgrade</strong> button in the right-hand column. You’ll land on the plan comparison page that lists every tier above your current one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan and pay">
    Pick the higher plan, review the prorated amount, and pay the invoice. Once the payment clears, the upgrade finalises automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2 — Order from the Upgrades tab

Need to compare plan details first? Use the dedicated Upgrades tab.

![Upgrade plan selector in the tab view](/assets/Upgrade-newplan.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Upgrades tab">
    In the service view, click <strong>Upgrade</strong> in the tabs bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Upgrade Plan">
    Press the blue <strong>Upgrade Your Plan</strong> button. This loads the comparison page that lists each higher tier with resource details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and checkout">
    Compare CPU, RAM, storage, and traffic quotas. Select your target plan, pay the prorated invoice, and follow the activation email and the steps below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Back up important data and schedule downtime. After payment, follow the activation email and perform a full power cycle to load CPU/RAM changes; a restart inside the operating system is not the same operation.</Warning>

---

## After the Upgrade

- **IP & data** stay the same—no migration is required.
- **RAM & vCPU** are available after a full power cycle from the EDBB panel.
- **Disk size** increases, but filesystems and partitions are untouched; expand them manually if you want to use the extra space.
- **Traffic allowance** increases immediately.

If you only need more RAM, CPU, disk space or IP addresses, use Upgrade Components.

[Upgrade components individually](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-resources)
