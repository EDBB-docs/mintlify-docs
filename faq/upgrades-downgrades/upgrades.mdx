<span id="choose-the-right-upgrade-strategy" />

Choose an upgrade based on the resource that is limiting your workload. Back up important data and plan for activation downtime before ordering.

| Need | Path |
| --- | --- |
| A larger allocation of several resources | [Upgrade the plan](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-plan) |
| Additional RAM, vCPU, disk or IP addresses | [Upgrade individual resources](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-resources) |
| More virtual disk space | [Drive Boost](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-disk-drive-boost) |
| Temporary additional data transfer | [Traffic Pool](/faq/traffic-bandwidth/traffic-pool) |

Review current EDBB prices and resulting resource totals before payment. Plan and component upgrades are prorated for the remaining billing period. EDBB plans have globally uniform pricing; EDIS Global plan names and allowances do not apply.

Follow the activation email. CPU/RAM changes require a full panel power cycle, while guest disk expansion is a separate task. See [upgrade data safety](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/upgrade-data-safety).

In-place downgrades are not supported. To move to a smaller service, plan a tested [migration](/faq/upgrades-downgrades/downgrade-policy) rather than assuming an image from a larger disk will fit.
