EDBB does not offer a free VPS trial. Eligible new VPS purchases may qualify for a refund under the [24-hour refund policy](/faq/billing-lifecycle/cancellation-and-refund-policy).

The request must reach support within **24 hours of purchase** and meet all eligibility conditions. Only one refund per customer is available in a 12-month period. Added Pool Traffic, upgrades, completed location changes and other listed exclusions can remove eligibility.

Before ordering, compare [EDBB plans and locations](https://www.edbb.com/) and run the [available network tests](/faq/ip-addresses/ping-test-ip). Choose resources appropriate for your software and understand that EDBB VPS hosting is unmanaged: you administer the operating system, security and backups.
