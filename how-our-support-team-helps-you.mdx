## What support covers

We help with invoices, payments, service availability and EDBB panel functions such as reinstalling, traffic assignment and rDNS. We can review infrastructure symptoms and point you to the relevant troubleshooting guide.

<span id="bring-in-a-systems-expert" />

EDBB VPS services are unmanaged. You administer the guest operating system and applications, including installation, patching, configuration, account access, migrations and backups. For hands-on guest administration, use your own administrator or a trusted managed-service provider.

<span id="get-the-help-you-need" />

## Prepare a useful request

Include the service ID or IP address, the time and timezone of the issue, the error and the checks you already performed. Send relevant logs with secrets removed. Supply your [Support PIN](/vps-management/your-support-pin) only through the official support channel when needed for verification; do not post it in a public issue.

Check [shared infrastructure status](https://status.edis.global/) for known incidents. For abuse reports, use [abuse@edbb.com](mailto:abuse@edbb.com).
